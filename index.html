FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

How do you organise bulk items?
We use consolidation services and dedicated warehouse space to organise bulk shipments. Items are grouped by destination, size and handling needs to make the best use of container space and reduce costs.
What is the estimated delivery time?
It depends on the method and destination: express air takes 1–3 days, standard air 3–7 days, sea freight 15–45 days and land transport 3–14 days. You can follow every stage of your shipment online.
Do you provide insurance?
Yes. Every shipment includes basic insurance, and premium cover of up to 100% of cargo value is available for high-value or fragile items. Our claims process is quick and straightforward.
How can I track my shipment?
Enter your tracking ID in the search box above or on the tracking page. Customers can also sign in to see their shipments, photos, updates and history.
